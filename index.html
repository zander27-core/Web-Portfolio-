About Me

Zander Estanislao — Legazpi, Philippines

A student who builds things end to end.

I'm a 3rd year Information Technology student based in the Philippines, currently deepening my skills in full-stack web development and human-centered design. What started as curiosity about "how websites actually work" turned into a habit of building — POS systems, campus tools, community platforms — anything that solves a real problem for real people.

I care about the details most users never notice: a form that validates cleanly, a layout that doesn't break on a small screen, a query that doesn't crawl. Outside of coursework, I explore UI/UX design principles and contribute to small personal projects to keep building.
